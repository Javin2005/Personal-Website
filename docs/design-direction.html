Editable, not hard-coded

Every word, color and font above lives in the database. Colors and fonts become CSS variables the whole site reads, so changing a color in Admin recolors the site. Sections can be hidden and reordered.

Login screen

Stays terminal-style, but toned down: no blinking icon, no "ACCESS DENIED" theatrics, no "Execute_Login". It will use the new fonts and colors.

Motion

One designed moment: the hero video dims and drifts slightly as you scroll away. Links and project rows get a small hover shift in the accent color. No fade-in-on-scroll on every section.

Lighter page

The video gets re-encoded smaller and pauses when it's off screen. Your profile photo gets resized from 3.8 MB to around 100 KB.
